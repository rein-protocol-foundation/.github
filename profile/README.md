# Rein Protocol Foundation

**Making AI agents safe for people and society.**

Rein Protocol Foundation brings together researchers, developers, and communities to advance safer AI agents through open-source tools, research, and shared learning. We focus on making agent behavior understandable, permissions explicit, and human oversight practical.

[Website](https://rein-protocol.org) · [Community](https://rein-protocol.org/community) · [Programs](https://rein-protocol.org/programs)

## What we work on

- **Agent safety and oversight:** transparency, observability, policy controls, and mechanisms for human intervention.
- **Open research and infrastructure:** reusable tools, reproducible research artifacts, and resources that help others inspect and build on the work.
- **Community and learning:** meetups, workshops, technical discussions, and shared learning materials for people building and studying AI agents.

We are an AI-native organization: agents help with research, coordination, and operations within defined limits. People set direction and remain accountable. Learn more about our [governance](https://rein-protocol.org/governance).

## Get involved

We welcome contributions to research, software, documentation, learning resources, and community activities.

- For a repository-specific improvement, read its README and contribution guidance, then open an issue or pull request.
- [Propose a project or community activity](https://rein-protocol.org/community/contribute).
- [Share a learning resource](https://rein-protocol.org/community/contribute/resources/submit).
- [Apply to contribute](https://rein-protocol.org/community/contribute/apply) if you would like to take responsibility for ongoing work.

Please follow our [Code of Conduct](https://rein-protocol.org/community/code-of-conduct). Check each repository for its own licensing and reuse terms.
